2 Are there any differences between the food people eat today and the food people ate in the past?
现在人们吃的食物和过去有什么不同？
Yes, there are big differences. In the past, people mainly ate seasonal and local food, because there was little choice and no cold storage, so meals were simple. Today, we can buy food from all over the world at any time, and processed and delivered food is everywhere. People’s eating habits have also changed: many people eat out or order takeaway instead of cooking at home. However, some people now care more about healthy, natural food, which is a kind of return to the past.
3 Do people in different regions eat the same special food every day?
不同地区的人每天都吃同样的特色食物吗？
No, not at all. China is huge, so different regions have completely different daily food. People in the south mainly eat rice, while people in the north prefer wheat food like noodles and steamed buns. The taste also differs: Sichuan food is famous for being spicy, Cantonese food is light, and food in the northwest often features lamb and bread. So regional food depends on local climate, farming and history.
4 What type of cuisine do people like in China?
中国人喜欢什么类型的菜系？
Chinese people love many types of cuisine. Home-style dishes are always popular, because they are simple and familiar. Sichuan cuisine is liked across the country for its spicy, rich flavour, and Cantonese food is popular for its fresh ingredients and light taste. Besides Chinese food, young people also enjoy Western fast food and Japanese or Korean dishes, so the modern Chinese table really mixes everything.
5 What are the differences between everyday food and festival food?
日常食物和节日食物有什么区别？
Everyday food is simple, quick and cheap, because people need it for energy during busy days. Festival food, on the other hand, is richer and more carefully prepared, with more meat, more dishes and traditional items that are only made on special days. Festival food often has symbolic meaning as well, like fish for surplus and dumplings for wealth. Also, everyday meals are often eaten quickly or alone, while festival meals bring the whole family together around one table.
6 What's the advantages and disadvantages of cooking at home?
在家做饭有什么优点和缺点？
Cooking at home has many advantages: it’s cheaper than eating out, the ingredients are fresh and clean, and you can control the oil and salt, so the food is healthier. For many people, cooking is also a relaxing hobby. However, home cooking takes time and energy, because you need to shop, prepare and wash up, which is hard after a long day at work. Also, not everyone knows how to cook well, so the food may not taste as good as restaurant dishes.
7 Do you prefer eating at home or outside?
你更喜欢在家吃饭还是在外面吃？
I prefer eating at home most of the time. Home food is healthier, because I know exactly what goes into the dishes, and it’s also much cheaper than eating outside. Besides, eating at home feels comfortable and relaxed, especially with family. But I do enjoy eating outside occasionally, such as on birthdays or when I meet friends, because restaurants offer a wider choice and a different atmosphere. So my answer is: home food for daily life, outside food for special moments.
