## II. Speaking (11-14 minutes)

Part 1 is about 4–5 minutes (2–3 sets of topics, 11–14 questions) and Part 2 is about 1 minute after the card is given, about 2 minutes for presentation, and about 4–5 minutes for abstract questioning on the card theme.

### Part 1 - Three-story formula (approximately 15 seconds each)

1. Answer directly (Yes / Not really + Core sentence)
2. Reason or details (cause.../ a specific scene)
3. Additional extensions (but... / when I... / for extension...)

> Yes/ No; 2 pass, 3 is good.

### Part 2 — Four Steps to Narrative

1. First sentence location:<span style="color:#e5484d">I'd like to talk about…</span>(WHO/ WHOT+ Time and place)
2. 2-3 sentences by card 4 (** prompt card order is your outline**)
3. End of feeling:<span style="color:#e5484d">Honestly, what I remember most is…</span>
4. Prepare to write only 4 words in 1 minute, without sentence

### Part 3 — 3-4 sentence formula

Point of view → reason → exemplification/comparative sentence:<span style="color:#3b82f6">I'd say that…</span> <span style="color:#3b82f6">It depends on…</span> <span style="color:#3b82f6">The reason is that…</span> <span style="color:#3b82f6">whereas</span> <span style="color:#3b82f6">especially when…</span> <span style="color:#3b82f6">like when I…</span>

### 4 Bottom Lines of Speech (6 for life, 7 plus points)

1. ** Answer to questions** ** — The examiner asked what to answer, not the questions he was prepared to ask.
2. Allow to think 1-2 seconds<span style="color:#3b82f6">That's a good question.</span>But once is enough.
3. Natural vocabulary > misbacked advanced word
4. Syntax: Old-fashioned, third-person singular, quantifiable/unnumberable
