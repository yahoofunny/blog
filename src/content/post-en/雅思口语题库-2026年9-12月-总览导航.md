### Please describe this information briefly.

The collection is produced by the Study Group of the Ducks Expedition Examination, which has been updated quarterly since 2013 and has been in existence for 13 years. In January, May and early September, the Yassi talk card is replaced by about 30 per cent – 50 per cent: last season's new topics were kept in this season, while new ones were added and the rest were phased out.

### IDP applies? Overseas?

Application. The IDP applies to all BC examinations, as do overseas examination regions such as mainland and South-East Asia.

### Practice Path Proposal <span class="wm-star" aria-hidden="true"></span>

1. ** Part 1**:4 A question-by-issue 3-4 sentence is written, followed by a new one (approximately 2-3 topic groups, 11-14 questions each).
2. **Part 2**: Test-paced cards — one minute after the card is drawn and only points are noted, followed by a continuous presentation of approximately two minutes, covering all four tip points of the card; it is recommended that a five-type material repository be built to ensure that any card is fully presented.
3. ** Part 3**: the examiner will be asking for approximately 4-5 minutes on the same card subject, and prepare four types of "causes/ comparison/change/ pros and cons" in the direction of Part 2.
4. Audio echoes, focusing on correction of the current, third-person singular, plural and pronunciation.
