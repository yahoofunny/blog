## 1.1 Circuits and circuit models

The circuits are circuits made of electrical equipment and components that are connected in a certain way. The circuit model is an approximation of the actual circuit using ideal components (R, L, C, power, etc.). Three components of circuits:** power supply** (provision of electricity),** load** (consumption of electricity),** middle link** (conductor, switch etc.).
